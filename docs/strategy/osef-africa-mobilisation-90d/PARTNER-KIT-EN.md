# OSEF — Partner Kit (EN)

**Working draft — STRAT-01 · 18 November 2026.** External distribution requires OSEF approval. No partnership or production-readiness claims without supporting evidence.

## Positioning

**Open Source Enterprise Factory (OSEF)** brings together engineering skills from French-speaking Africa to help organizations **advise, build, and operate** enterprise Open Source solutions.

**ADVISE · BUILD · OPERATE** — from needs assessment to an operable, documented service, including skills transfer and upstream contribution where appropriate.

**Value proposition:** “Your platform. Your teams. Your data. Our expertise.” This slogan does not constitute a contractual guarantee of data residency or ownership.

## Who we want to work with

| Audience | Need to explore | First collaboration |
|---|---|---|
| IT service providers / integrators | Additional Open Source delivery expertise | Joint architecture workshop and demonstrator |
| African cloud providers | Credible enterprise use cases on hosted infrastructure | A time-boxed OSEF Partner Lab |
| CIOs / CTOs | Support, risk management, continuity, reversibility | Demo, assessment or scoped pilot |
| Universities / communities | Practical learning and Open Source contribution | Contributor labs and mentoring |
| Open Source projects / foundations | Documentation, testing and upstream improvements | Issues and PRs with transparent status |

## Two initial demonstrators

### OSEF Essential Digital Workplace
**Target components:** Nextcloud, ONLYOFFICE and email services. The actual readiness, interoperability, security and deployment status must be checked before customer-facing claims.

Repository: https://github.com/opensource-enterprise-factory/osef-essentiel-digital-workplace

### OSEF Support Enterprise
A **target service offering still being industrialized**, built around Zammad: user portal, tickets, groups, SLAs, workflows and reporting, with optional integrations subject to validation.

**Not yet to be represented as an operational production service.** Planned delivery paths: On-Premise and Private Managed first; SaaS only after required operational, security and client-isolation checks.

Repository: https://github.com/opensource-enterprise-factory/osef-support-enterprise

## Suggested engagement model

1. **Discovery (25–30 minutes):** use case, stakeholders, constraints and desired outcome.
2. **Scope:** technical boundaries, resources, responsibilities, privacy, licensing and success criteria.
3. **Partner Lab:** isolated deployment, testing, measurements and documented limitations.
4. **Review:** go / improve / stop based on evidence.
5. **Commercialization:** only after mutually agreed service levels, pricing, liability and reversibility.

## Mutual contributions

**OSEF may contribute:** scoped engineering capacity, BDAT architecture, versioned automation, testing and operational documentation.

**The partner would contribute:** an identified need, an authorized point of contact, bounded lab resources and explicit approval of the test terms.

## Guardrails

- No unverified SLAs, certifications, prices, endorsements or availability guarantees.
- No partner logos or names used as references without permission.
- No real customer data in public demos.
- A successful lab does not equal production qualification.
- Keep commercial work distinct from independent communities and foundations.
