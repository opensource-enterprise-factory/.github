# OSEF WORLD TOUR 2027
## International Sponsorship & Partnership Prospectus
**From Francophone Africa to the Global Open Source Ecosystem**

**Period:** October–November 2027  
**Organization:** Open Source Enterprise Factory (OSEF)  
**Program:** OSEF Africa Global Delegation 2027  
**Operational Base:** Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire  
**Status:** Partnership proposal — open for discussion.

> Events, budgets, targets, amounts and sponsorship benefits are proposals. No partner, funding, speaking opportunity or travel participation is represented as confirmed.

## 1. Executive Summary

Open Source Enterprise Factory (OSEF) is developing an engineering initiative to turn open source technologies into enterprise-ready solutions while strengthening the capabilities and contributions of engineers across Francophone Africa.

The **OSEF World Tour 2027** proposes structured international engagement among African engineers, project maintainers, open source foundations and technology companies.

The program focuses on open source engineering and upstream contributions, Cloud Native and Platform Engineering, AI and open infrastructure, software supply chain security, enterprise adoption, partnerships and knowledge transfer.

**Our request:** sponsor targeted international missions, public reproducible labs, engineer contribution pathways, and community knowledge-transfer workshops. This is not simply a conference travel program.

## 2. About OSEF

**Mission:** Help organizations adopt, integrate, operate and improve enterprise open source technologies while building a sustainable ecosystem of African engineers and contributors.

### ADVISE
Open source strategy and governance, enterprise architecture, OSPO practices, technical assessment, digital sovereignty and resilience.

### BUILD
Digital Workplace, enterprise IT support, Cloud Native platforms, Kubernetes and GitOps, AI infrastructure, automation and secure software delivery.

### OPERATE
SRE, observability, security operations, infrastructure automation, incident management, support and SLAs.

### Engineering initiatives
- **OSEF Essentiel Digital Workplace:** an open source collaboration and document-management initiative.
- **OSEF Support Enterprise:** an open source IT support and workflow automation initiative.
- **Cloud Native & Platform Engineering Labs:** practical Kubernetes, GitOps, reliability and security labs.
- **OSEF Tech Intelligence:** technology intelligence translated into tests, labs, contributions and knowledge sharing.

Project maturity and achieved outcomes must be supported by a separate technical evidence portfolio.

## 3. Why the World Tour?

1. **Connect African engineers** directly with international project maintainers and communities.
2. **Increase upstream contributions:** pull requests, tests, documentation, security improvements and reproducible bug reports.
3. **Accelerate enterprise adoption** through demonstrations, technical assessments and workshops.
4. **Develop international partnerships** between African engineering communities and global organizations.
5. **Transfer knowledge locally** through reusable resources and post-mission sessions.

## 9. Alternative Partnership Models

1. **Sponsored Engineering Challenge:** a partner defines a scoped open source problem; OSEF builds a reproducible proof of concept or assessment under agreement.
2. **Sponsored Knowledge Transfer:** a partner funds workshops for African engineers and organizations following international conferences.
3. **Sponsored Contributor Development:** a partner supports an eligible engineer with mentorship, labs and relevant international participation.

Outputs will respect open source licensing, confidentiality, security and intellectual property requirements.

## 11. Accountability and Impact Reporting

**Technical:** labs, submitted and merged PRs, documentation and reproducible evidence.  
**Community:** workshops, documented attendance, educational materials and mentoring.  
**Partnerships:** meetings, follow-ups and formal agreements where applicable.  
**Financial:** approved budget, documented spending, variances and resource use.

Personal and confidential information will not be disclosed without authorization.

## 12. Partnership Governance

Every partnership requires written terms covering scope, deliverables, responsibilities, compliance and conflict-of-interest checks, approved branding, open source license obligations, confidentiality, reporting and closure.

**The contracting legal entity, payment arrangements and jurisdiction must be clarified before signature.**
