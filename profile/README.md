## Our mission

**Open Source Enterprise Factory (OSEF)** is an emerging pan-African engineering initiative bringing together software engineers, infrastructure specialists, architects, security practitioners, platform engineers, and technology leaders.

We believe African organizations should do more than consume open source technology. They should be able to **understand it, evaluate it, integrate it, secure it, operate it, improve it, and contribute upstream**.

Our purpose is to connect **real enterprise challenges** with **hands-on engineering**, building practical expertise and reusable solutions for organizations across Africa.

> From technology to value. From adoption to contribution.

## How we work

### 1. DISCOVER — Listen to enterprises
We investigate how open source is used in African information systems, where it is business-critical, and what organizations need to operate it confidently.

### 2. DESIGN — Make sound engineering choices
We document requirements, architecture decisions, security controls, operational constraints, and technology trade-offs.

### 3. BUILD — Prove it in labs
We develop reproducible reference architectures, automation, integration examples, tests, and technical documentation.

### 4. DEPLOY — Engineer for real operations
We focus on reliability, observability, security, maintainability, recovery, and knowledge transfer—not just installation.

### 5. CONTRIBUTE — Give back upstream
We aim to improve the projects we rely on through documentation, issue triage, translations, tests, bug fixes, code, and community participation.

**Our quality principle:** a solution is not proven merely because it works once. Another engineer should be able to reproduce, understand, secure, operate, and recover it.

## Our open source engineering labs

OSEF Labs are collaborative workspaces for learning, validation, and reusable engineering outputs. Potential lab tracks include:

- **Infrastructure & Virtualization Lab** — Linux, hypervisors, private cloud, storage, networking.
- **Cloud Native & Platform Lab** — Kubernetes, GitOps, automation, developer platforms.
- **Security & Identity Lab** — IAM, software supply chain, hardening, secrets, resilience.
- **Data & AI Lab** — databases, data engineering, open AI infrastructure.
- **Enterprise Applications Lab** — digital workplace, ITSM, integration, interoperability.
- **Open Source Advisory Lab** — adoption assessments, governance, licensing, technology radar.

Labs will publish their scope, maintainers, reproducible instructions, evidence, and contribution opportunities as they become available.

## Our commitment to upstream communities

We respect the maintainers, contributors, licenses, governance models, and codes of conduct of the projects we use.

Our contributions may include:

- Documentation, tutorials, and French-language accessibility.
- Reproducible bug reports, tests, and interoperability improvements.
- Security and reliability improvements coordinated responsibly.
- Code contributions and maintenance, aligned with upstream project needs.
- Sharing practical lessons from African production environments without exposing confidential information.

**Contribution quality matters more than contribution counts.** We distinguish proposed work, submitted pull requests, and changes accepted upstream.

## Who can join?

We welcome contributors across Francophone Africa and beyond:

**Software engineers · Linux and network engineers · DevOps and SRE practitioners · Platform engineers · Security specialists · Database engineers · AI/data engineers · Solution architects · Technical writers · Open source strategists · Students and mentors.**

You do not need a certification to contribute. Curiosity, professionalism, collaboration, and a willingness to learn are what matter.

## Get involved

1. **Explore** the repositories and open issues in this organization.
2. **Introduce yourself** through an available discussion or issue when the relevant repository enables one.
3. **Start small:** improve documentation, reproduce an issue, propose a test, or review an existing contribution.
4. **Read each repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md`** before opening a pull request.
5. **Share a real-world problem** that could become a documented, reproducible open source lab.

**Organization:** https://github.com/opensource-enterprise-factory

If a repository or contribution channel is not yet available, check back as the organization develops.

## Our principles

- **Open collaboration:** work in the open whenever confidentiality and security permit.
- **Engineering rigor:** favor reproducibility, documentation, testing, and peer review.
- **Security by design:** treat security and resilience as core engineering requirements.
- **Respect for upstream:** contribute according to project rules and maintainers' priorities.
- **African relevance, global standards:** solve local problems without compromising technical excellence.
- **Integrity:** be transparent about maturity, affiliations, capabilities, and outcomes.
- **Knowledge transfer:** create opportunities for more African engineers to build lasting expertise.
